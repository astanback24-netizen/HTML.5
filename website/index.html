Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer Maryland HQL training? +

Yes, absolutely! We are fully certified to provide complete Maryland HQL training. This course covers all state-required curriculum, including firearm safety, operation, and Maryland law.

I'm a complete beginner. Is your training right for me? +

Yes! We specialize in creating a patient, safe, and non-judgmental environment for beginners. We start with the 4 universal safety rules and build your confidence from the ground up.

Where are you located? +

We offer two great options: private, in-home classes where we come to your location, or live, interactive classes held over Zoom for your convenience.

What are your certifications? +

Our instructors are certified by the USCCA (U.S. Concealed Carry Association) as Certified Firearms Instructors and Certified Range Safety Officers.
